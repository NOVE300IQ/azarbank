About AzarBank

A fictional digital bank created as a front-end learning project.

Our Story

AzarBank started as a simple idea while learning web development. I wanted to create something that looked like a real digital banking website while practicing HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

The project slowly grew from a basic landing page into a small multi-page website with animations and interactive features.

What We Believe

Banking should be simple and easy to understand. Good design should help people find what they need instead of making things more complicated.

Simple

Easy to understand and easy to use.

Modern

A clean experience built for the web.

Accessible

Designed to work across different devices.
